Fertilisation

  • Fertilisation happens when ONE sperm fuses with ONE egg cell.
  • It takes place in the tube (oviduct) between the ovary and the womb.
  • Millions of sperms are released, but only one enters the egg. After that, no other sperm can enter.

Development

2. Growing in the womb

Open full page

Key ideas

  • The fertilised egg attaches to the wall of the womb and divides many times.
  • It develops into an embryo, then a fetus, over about 9 months.
  • The womb protects the fetus; the fluid around it cushions it from bumps.
  • Through the umbilical cord, the fetus gets digested food and oxygen from the mother, and its waste is removed.

Try it

  • Wait one month at a time and watch the stats: length and “size of”. When does the embryo become a fetus? When does the fetus turn head down?
Heredity

3. Traits from both parents

Open full page

Key ideas

  • Offspring inherit traits from BOTH parents.
  • The genetic information is in the nucleus of the sperm and of the egg (recall the cell chapter: the nucleus controls the cell).
  • Brothers and sisters get different mixes, so they do not look exactly alike.

6. Practice questions

Practice 1: What is fertilisation?

Fertilisation is the fusion of the nucleus of one sperm (male reproductive cell) with the nucleus of one egg cell (female reproductive cell).

Practice 2: State the function of the ovaries and of the testes.

The ovaries produce egg cells. The testes produce sperms.

Practice 3: How does the womb help the developing fetus?

It protects the fetus, and it provides it with nutrients (digested food) and oxygen through the umbilical cord.

Practice 4: Mei has dimples like her father and straight hair like her mother. Explain why.

She inherited traits from both parents. The genetic information in the nucleus of the sperm came from her father, and in the nucleus of the egg from her mother.

Practice 5: Two brothers have the same parents but look different. Why?

Each child gets a different mix of genetic information from the two parents, so they inherit different combinations of traits.
