What is a cell?

1. Under the microscope

Open full page

Key ideas

  • A cell is the microscopic functional unit of all living things. Most cells can only be seen with a microscope.
  • Unicellular organisms are made of one cell, e.g. bacteria, amoeba.
  • Multicellular organisms are made of many cells, e.g. humans, trees.

Try it

  • Turn the focus knob to see the onion cells clearly. Then try each slide. Which ones have a cell wall? Which have chloroplasts? Which are unicellular?
Question: Why do onion skin cells have no chloroplasts?

The onion bulb grows underground and is not exposed to light, so its cells do not carry out photosynthesis.

Question: How can you tell the cheek cells are animal cells?

They have no cell wall, so their shape is irregular. They also have no chloroplasts.

2. Plant cell and animal cell

ChloroplastsNoYes (green parts only)Contain chlorophyll to trap sunlight for making food.
Exam watch-out: do ALL plant cells have chloroplasts? No! Root cells, onion bulb cells and cells inside the stem do not have chloroplasts. They are not exposed to light and do not carry out photosynthesis.

Try it

  • Pick each part. Change the plant cell to a root or onion cell and pick chloroplast. Press both cells: the animal cell squashes, the plant cell keeps its shape.
Question: When both cells were pressed, why did only the animal cell change shape?

The plant cell has a rigid cell wall that gives it shape and support. The animal cell has no cell wall.

Cell division

3. Cells divide: growth and repair

Open full page

Key ideas

  • Growth and repair happen when cells divide to form new cells. One cell divides into two.
  • A cut heals because the cells beside it divide and fill the gap.
  • A child grows taller because cells divide and the new cells grow.

Try it

  • Make a cut, then press Wait 1 day twice. Watch the cell count go from 32 down to 24 and back to 32.

Exam skills

5. Practice questions

Practice 1: A cell has a nucleus, cytoplasm, a cell membrane and a cell wall, but no chloroplasts. Is it a plant or animal cell? Explain.

It is a plant cell, because it has a cell wall. It has no chloroplasts because it is from a part of the plant not exposed to light, such as the root.

Practice 2: Why do leaf cells have chloroplasts but root cells do not?

Leaf cells are exposed to light. Their chloroplasts contain chlorophyll to trap light energy to make food. Root cells are underground, are not exposed to light and do not make food.

Practice 3: Which part of the cell would you remove to stop it from controlling its activities? Why?

The nucleus. It controls all the activities of the cell and contains the genetic material.

Practice 4: A plant cell keeps its shape even when the plant is pressed. Explain.

The plant cell has a rigid cell wall that gives it a fixed shape and support.

Practice 5: How does a cut on your finger heal?

The cells around the cut divide to form new cells. The new cells fill the gap and repair the skin.
